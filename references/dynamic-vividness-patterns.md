## Traffic accident / incidental collision pattern

A useful literary example is Luo Ji's traffic accident sequence in Liu Cixin's *The Dark Forest*.

Study:

- mundane social interaction and body placement;
- a small action changes spatial relation;
- an independently moving traffic process intersects with that change;
- the body experiences the event before fully interpreting it;
- the environment remains present rather than dissolving into abstract danger.

Mechanism: **incidental contact + kinetic causality + limited perception**.

## Fragmented participation in a large event

Stendhal's Waterloo material in *The Charterhouse of Parma* offers a useful pattern:

- large historical process is experienced through local fragments;
- the participant lacks panoramic understanding;
- banal, confusing, frightening details coexist;
- event significance exceeds what the viewpoint can recognize in the moment.

Mechanism: **local experience inside a larger process**.

## Distributed causality under crisis

Tolstoy's battle and military-life writing offers a useful pattern:

- bodily contingency and habit matter;
- local units act with incomplete control;
- many partial actions combine into outcomes no single actor fully commands;
- ordinary routines persist near danger.

Mechanism: **distributed causality + anti-command-center realism**.

## Shared stimulus, divergent interpretation

The motor-car interruption in Virginia Woolf's *Mrs Dalloway* offers a useful pattern:

- one public event interrupts urban flow;
- different people interpret it through different fears, assumptions, and private concerns;
- the city becomes a network of simultaneous attention rather than a backdrop.

Mechanism: **public-field propagation**.

## Urban simultaneity and crossing lives

The "Wandering Rocks" episode of Joyce's *Ulysses* offers a useful pattern:

- multiple lives proceed simultaneously in one city;
- recurring people and objects cross separate local scenes;
- the city is understood through partial intersections rather than one protagonist's ownership.

Mechanism: **offscreen simultaneity + spatial cross-threading**.

Dos Passos's *Manhattan Transfer* offers a related pattern:

- transit and urban motion organize fragmented lives;
- shared infrastructure links otherwise separate trajectories;
- the city can feel autonomous without being given one literal intention.

Mechanism: **networked urban motion**.

## Physical/logistical concreteness in large-scale crisis

The Caporetto retreat in Hemingway's *A Farewell to Arms* offers a useful pattern:

- obstacles, mud, vehicles, delays, and bodily work carry the reality;
- interpretation stays restrained while constraints accumulate;
- large war becomes immediate practical problems.

Mechanism: **material logistics as lived history**.

## Foreground/background coexistence

Alfonso Cuaron's *Children of Men* offers a useful audiovisual analogue:

- foreground action continues while populated background action persists;
- onscreen and implied offscreen space remain active;
- unrelated social reality does not vanish during danger;
- the world feels larger than the hero's current objective.

Mechanism: **continuous foreground action inside autonomous public space**.
