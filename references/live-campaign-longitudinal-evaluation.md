## 1. Purpose

The deterministic 60-100 chapter benchmark proves that continuity machinery can survive long synthetic histories. It does **not** prove that the Skill remains useful, natural, recoverable, and creatively healthy during months of real drafting.

A live campaign is longitudinal evidence gathered from an actual creative project as chapters are genuinely planned, drafted, revised, accepted, resumed, and sometimes retconned across sessions.

The goal is diagnostic evidence, not a leaderboard score.

## 2. Non-substitution rule

Never promote synthetic evidence into a live-campaign claim.

Synthetic / generated stress projects may:

- test replay and schema stability;
- test context-window retirement;
- test delayed promise/custody/knowledge continuity;
- exercise mutation, metamorphic, and fuzz gates.

They may not establish:

- real 50/100/200/300-chapter writing evidence;
- sustained prose vitality;
- human author usability over months;
- real maintenance burden;
- cold-resume quality under genuine forgetting;
- originality or non-flattening over an evolving book.

The built-in `LWA-LONG-HORIZON-*` benchmark project family is explicitly ineligible for live-campaign claims.

## 3. What counts as a live campaign

A campaign may be registered as `live-creative-campaign` only when it has:

1. a real project with ordinary chapter files produced through the author's actual creative workflow;
2. durable project state and, where enabled, Runtime history generated by real chapter work;
3. distinct drafting/resume sessions rather than one batch generator pretending to be longitudinal use;
4. trace-grounded checkpoints that hash the artifacts that actually existed at that point;
5. at least some human/author review rather than only self-judging automation;
6. explicit provenance for synthetic assistance, imported material, or benchmark-derived probes;
7. no claim stronger than the recorded evidence supports.

A campaign protocol cannot cryptographically prove that a human genuinely wrote or reviewed something. It can make evidence auditable and make false substitution harder. Treat provenance claims as attestations plus artifact evidence, not metaphysical proof.

## 4. Registration anchor and session ledger

Treat a live campaign as a prospective observation process, not a retrospective scrapbook. `campaign init` records a harness-generated UTC registration time, the project chapter visible at registration, and hashes of the project/control-plane artifacts that existed then. User-supplied `created_at` is descriptive attestation only; it cannot move the registration anchor backward.

The chapter horizon is **registration-relative**. If a real novel already has 50 accepted chapters when instrumentation begins, that chapter is the baseline, not retroactive evidence. A 100-chapter campaign then observes chapters 51 through 150, with canonical checkpoints at 51, 60, 75, 100, 125, and 150. `target_chapters` therefore means future observed campaign chapters, not the novel's absolute final chapter number. The project must never move behind the registration chapter while the campaign is active.

A checkpoint must reference a session that was registered earlier with `campaign session-start`. The harness captures that start itself. A free-form `session_id` inside checkpoint JSON is not evidence of a distinct session.

Use `campaign session-end` when practical. Session outcomes are preserved as one of `completed`, `aborted`, `recovery-required`, `no-chapter`, or `other`. Do not discard aborted or repair-heavy sessions merely because they produced no accepted chapter. Longitudinal evidence becomes misleading when only successful sessions survive in the ledger.

The protocol cannot prove that every real writing session was logged, so the manifest declares a `session_logging_scope`:

- `checkpoint-linked-only` — only sessions supporting campaign evidence are guaranteed to be instrumented;
- `all-creative-sessions` — the author attests that all creative sessions in the observed campaign are being logged.

The second claim is still an attestation, not cryptographic proof. Report it as scope, never as certainty.

## 5. Longitudinal evidence model

Evaluate the **trajectory**, not only the final chapter.

Each checkpoint records four evidence planes.

### A. Trace evidence

- chapter file hash and size;
- Project Manifest and Story State hashes;
- Runtime event head/count when Runtime is active;
- current branch and context generation;
- Writer Pack manifest/hash when available;
- relevant revision/reconciliation artifacts;
- hashed probe-evidence artifacts for every passing capability probe;
- hashed creative-evaluation artifacts when a fingerprint, voice-drift report, Project-Poetics artifact/observatory snapshot, or pairwise review is cited;
- Skill control-plane and campaign-harness hashes, so a months-long campaign does not silently mix incompatible evaluation versions.

### B. Capability evidence

- continuity/integrity checks;
- cold-resume recovery;
- memory-to-action use;
- update/interference handling;
- abstention on authorial unknowns;
- branch/secret isolation;
- stale-plan retirement.

### C. Burden evidence

- context entries/tokens and budget ratio;
- maintenance/repair actions;
- retcons and invalidations;
- manual recovery work;
- time or effort notes when the author elects to record them.

### D. Creative longitudinal evidence

- human/author checkpoint notes;
- blinded pairwise comparisons when useful;
- voice/architecture drift diagnostics, interpreted against authorized scoped Project Poetics;
- Project-Poetics lineage/override evidence when a project deliberately changes formal regime;
- repeated-scene/template warnings;
- novelty, surprise, or flattening concerns;
- whether the novel's style/characters evolve rather than freeze into a system-generated equilibrium.

Do not collapse all four planes into one score.

## 6. Canonical checkpoints

For a campaign registered before chapter 1, a target of 100 observed chapters collects at least:

`1, 10, 25, 50, 75, 100`

For 200 observed chapters, additionally collect offsets:

`150, 200`

For 300 observed chapters, additionally collect offsets:

`250, 300`

For an existing project registered at chapter `N`, add `N` to every offset. The maturity level is based on the observed horizon after registration, never on the absolute chapter number printed on the manuscript.

Extra checkpoints are encouraged after:

- a major retcon;
- branch creation/merge;
- model or agent change;
- long hiatus;
- governance-profile upgrade;
- migration across project/runtime versions;
- large cast/setting re-entry;
- major prose-style change.

Checkpoint spacing is deliberately non-uniform. Early checkpoints catch setup failures; later spacing measures persistence and accumulated interference.

## 7. What to measure

### Integrity

Record whether canonical owners pass:

- Project Doctor / manifest verification;
- Narrative Semantics Kernel;
- Runtime schema/replay when active;
- prose-state reconciliation when required;
- secret/context leakage checks relevant to the chapter.

### Long-gap continuity

Probe facts whose usefulness spans many chapters:

- character knowledge acquired before a long absence;
- object custody across transfers;
- injury/resource residue;
- institutional consequences;
- dormant promises and debts;
- changed rules/permissions;
- research facts whose freshness matters.

### Update fidelity and interference resistance

Do not only ask whether an old fact can be recalled. Test whether the system correctly prefers a later revision over an earlier state and resists semantically similar distractors.

Examples:

- an address changed in chapter 41; chapter 73 must use the new one;
- a character learned the truth in chapter 62; chapter 80 must not revert to earlier ignorance;
- two similarly named objects/locations must not exchange custody or properties.

### Reader and permission continuity

Check that:

- Reader Model reflects actual exposure rather than backend truth;
- Writer context does not gain future/branch-local secrets;
- declassification changes access without changing truth/provenance;
- stale chapter-local plans retire.

## 8. Memory-to-action probes and probe modes

Long-term memory matters only if it changes later decisions correctly.

Prefer **action probes** over pure trivia recall. Every probe also declares how it entered the workflow:

- `observational` — the dependency arose naturally from the real writing process and the later action would have been needed even without evaluation;
- `intervention` — the evaluator deliberately inserted or selected a challenge primarily to test the system;
- `recovery` — the probe exists specifically to test resume, migration, repair, or recovery behavior.

Do not merge these modes into one success rate. Intervention probes are valuable stress evidence but have weaker ecological validity. L4 and above therefore require at least one passing **observational action** probe; a campaign cannot establish naturalistic long-horizon usefulness using only evaluator-created quizzes.

Probe classes:

- `recall`: can the system retrieve the relevant durable fact?
- `action`: does that fact alter the next plan/scene/tool choice correctly?
- `update`: does the latest revision override the stale version?
- `interference`: can it distinguish similar entities/events?
- `abstention`: does it refuse to invent an unresolved authorial unknown?
- `permission`: does it avoid using information the active role cannot see?
- `reader`: does it plan reveals from reader exposure rather than backend knowledge?
- `branch`: does it act from the selected branch only?

A checkpoint should not count as strong long-horizon evidence if all probes are simple QA with no effect on action.

Every passing probe must reference a real project-relative evidence artifact. The campaign harness snapshots that artifact's path, size, and SHA-256 at checkpoint time. A prose sentence such as "the agent remembered correctly" is not artifact-grounded evidence.

When the probe tests a fact, state, promise, rule, or permission established earlier in the novel, record `source_chapter`. The checkpoint chapter then defines a demonstrated memory-action gap. Do not infer long-range memory from the chapter number alone: a chapter-100 action probe sourced from chapter 99 demonstrates a one-chapter gap, not a 99-chapter memory capability.

Keep **inherited pre-registration memory** separate from **campaign-origin memory**. A project registered at chapter 50 may legitimately demonstrate that it can recover a chapter-3 fact at chapter 75, but that is inherited-memory evidence: the campaign did not observe the fact when it entered the system. Report that raw gap descriptively. Strong live-campaign claims, including the required L4 observational action probe, must also contain evidence whose source chapter is after registration.

## 9. Cold-resume and interruption probes

At selected checkpoints, start a genuinely fresh session/agent context using only the project's documented recovery surface.

Record a structured `resume_probe`, including:

- the exact recovery files loaded, each hashed at checkpoint time;
- a project-relative evidence artifact describing the resume attempt;
- whether Project Manifest / Resume Capsule was sufficient;
- whether hidden truths remained isolated;
- whether the resumed agent chose the correct next action;
- whether it asked for already-known information;
- whether it resurrected stale plans;
- how much manual repair was needed.

A boolean `cold_resume=true` without this evidence is not a valid cold-resume checkpoint.

At least one cold-resume probe should follow a meaningful hiatus or simulated context loss. For stronger evidence, include a model/agent change while preserving the same project artifacts.

## 10. Creative drift and temporal flattening

Longitudinal correctness can coexist with creatively dead prose.

Current research on long sequences of LLM-generated text reports **temporal flattening**: generated trajectories can show less semantic and cognitive-emotional evolution than human writing trajectories even when local lexical diversity is high. Research on AI-assisted creativity also reports risks of homogenization and reduced originality under heavy generative assistance.

Therefore, campaign review should inspect trajectories rather than only local chapter quality.

Useful diagnostics:

- architecture/scene fingerprint recurrence;
- voice-drift curves by character and narrator;
- repeated conflict-resolution patterns;
- repeated paragraph/rhythm profiles;
- emotional-state variance across arcs;
- semantic novelty between distant chapter windows;
- whether side characters/institutions continue to surprise while remaining causal;
- whether every arc converges toward the same narrative geometry;
- whether apparent drift is unauthorized, or an explicitly scoped/approved Project-Poetics change.

Do **not** automatically punish stability. A deliberately stable narrator or recurring ritual may be correct. These diagnostics are prompts for human review, not a literary truth function.

## 11. Maintenance burden and bounded context

A long-horizon system can remain correct by becoming unusably expensive.

Track over checkpoints:

- Writer Pack entry count;
- estimated tokens and budget ratio;
- number of durable artifacts touched per chapter;
- repair/reconciliation actions;
- dependency invalidations;
- author interventions required to restore context;
- time/effort notes when available.

Look for growth curves, not isolated values. Use `campaign trajectory` to describe those curves without reducing them to one score.

Healthy long-horizon behavior should usually externalize durable state and keep the active Writer context bounded rather than allowing chapter number to imply unbounded context growth.

No universal token ceiling is imposed here. Different novels legitimately need different context budgets. The signal is unexplained growth, repeated emergency expansion, or loss of relevance.

## 12. Evidence passport: independent longitudinal axes

Do not let one impressive axis impersonate another. Each campaign exposes a descriptive **evidence passport** with multiple independent longitudinal axes.

### Chapter horizon

The existing `L0`-`L6` maturity levels answer: **how many real creative chapters have been prospectively observed since this campaign was registered?** They are registration-relative and intentionally ignore pre-registration chapter count.

They do not answer how much wall-clock time elapsed or how old the memories used by later actions actually were.

### Calendar horizon

The harness generates its own UTC `captured_at_utc` for each checkpoint. Author-supplied `recorded_at` remains an attestation, but calendar-span evidence is computed from harness capture times so a backdated timestamp cannot manufacture a months-long campaign.

Report calendar span descriptively using classes such as:

- `T0`: less than 1 day;
- `T1`: at least 1 day;
- `T7`: at least 7 days;
- `T30`: at least 30 days;
- `T90`: at least 90 days;
- `T180`: at least 180 days.

These are evidence descriptors, not quality grades. A fast author can legitimately reach chapter 100 with `T7`; the correct claim is then "100-chapter evidence over at least seven days", not "months-long evidence".

### Memory-to-action horizon

For probes with `source_chapter`, report the largest demonstrated source-to-checkpoint gap separately for all passed probes and for `action` probes specifically. Use descriptive classes `M0`, `M1`, `M10`, `M25`, `M50`, `M100`, and `M200`.

Do not force the plot to manufacture a long-gap dependency merely to raise this class. It is observational evidence: when a real long-gap dependency naturally exists, measure it honestly.

### Session horizon / observation coverage

Report instrumented sessions, closed sessions, unclosed sessions, and aborted/recovery-required outcomes separately. Distinct-session maturity counts only registered sessions referenced by eligible checkpoints. Arbitrary checkpoint `session_id` strings cannot manufacture session diversity.

The session ledger also exposes a basic missingness problem: an `all-creative-sessions` campaign with unexplained unclosed or omitted sessions deserves investigation, while `checkpoint-linked-only` makes no claim of complete observation. Do not silently interpret missing sessions as successful sessions.

### No composite score

A campaign might be `L4 / T30 / M50`: one hundred prospectively observed real chapters, at least thirty days of instrumented use, and at least one successful memory-grounded probe spanning fifty chapters. Another campaign might be `L4 / T0 / M1`. Both are valid records; they support very different claims. The evidence passport also reports campaign-origin memory horizons separately from raw gaps that may reach into pre-registration material.

Never average these axes into a single prestige number.

### Tooling eras and confounding

A months-long campaign may cross Skill, semantic-kernel, harness, model, or governance changes. Group consecutive checkpoints with the same tooling hashes into **tooling eras**. Treat comparisons across era boundaries as potentially confounded unless the campaign explicitly studies that transition.

This prevents a late improvement or regression from being silently attributed to long-term memory when the underlying Skill or evaluation harness changed midway.

## 13. Evidence maturity and claim gates

Campaign status is evidence-scoped.

### L0 — Protocol only

No live campaign evidence yet.

### L1 — Instrumented live campaign

- live project registered;
- at least the `registration chapter + 1` checkpoint;
- trace hashes valid;
- at least one human/author review record.

### L2 — Early longitudinal evidence

- reaches 25 observed chapters after registration;
- checkpoint offsets +1, +10, +25 present;
- at least two distinct sessions;
- at least one cold-resume or interruption probe;
- at least one memory-to-action probe.

### L3 — Mid-horizon evidence

- reaches chapter 50;
- checkpoints 1, 10, 25, 50 present;
- at least three distinct sessions;
- multiple human-reviewed checkpoints;
- action/update/interference or abstention probe coverage spans at least three probe classes.

### L4 — Real 100-chapter evidence

- reaches 100 observed chapters after registration;
- checkpoint offsets +1, +10, +25, +50, +75, +100 present;
- at least four distinct sessions;
- at least three human-reviewed checkpoints;
- at least two cold-resume/interruption probes;
- at least four distinct probe classes including `action`;
- at least one passing `observational` action probe arising from the ordinary creative workflow **and sourced after campaign registration**;
- no unresolved severe integrity blocker at the chapter-100 checkpoint.

### L5 — Real 200-chapter evidence

- L4 plus checkpoint offsets +150 and +200;
- at least six distinct sessions;
- at least four human-reviewed checkpoints;
- evidence of recovery after at least one major revision, hiatus, migration, or model/agent change.

### L6 — Real 300-chapter evidence

- L5 plus checkpoint offsets +250 and +300;
- at least eight distinct sessions;
- at least five human-reviewed checkpoints;
- longitudinal creative-drift review across multiple distant windows;
- evidence that maintenance burden/context growth remained manageable or that the system adapted transparently when it did not.

The CLI may verify artifact-backed requirements and count attestations. It cannot prove subjective human review happened in good faith. Claims must keep that limitation visible. `L0`-`L6` are **chapter-horizon evidence maturity**, never shorthand for calendar duration, literary quality, or demonstrated memory distance; use the evidence passport for those other axes.

## 14. Three-track campaign portfolio

To reduce overfitting to one narrative geometry, aim to accumulate at least three genuinely different campaigns over time:

1. **realist / mystery Persistent** — institutions, research freshness, hidden causality, reader exposure;
2. **xianxia / progression Persistent** — long growth curves, inventories/resources, factions, powers, multi-arc inheritance;
3. **time-loop / prophecy Forensic** — branch/counterfactual semantics, authorial unknowns, repeated scenes with different causal exposure.

Do not fabricate these campaigns merely to fill the portfolio. `assets/live-campaign/campaign-registry.json` starts as planned evidence and should advance only when real projects exist.

## 15. Current evidence status

The bundled Skill ships the protocol, harness, templates, and planned three-track registry only. It deliberately ships **no claimed L1-L6 live campaign result**. Until real projects accumulate prospective session/checkpoint evidence, the fourth moat layer remains infrastructure-ready but empirically at `L0`. Synthetic integration fixtures used to test this harness are never promoted into the campaign registry.

## 16. Data minimization and privacy

Real campaign evidence may contain unpublished manuscripts.

Prefer storing in the Skill only:

- schemas/protocols;
- anonymized checkpoint summaries;
- hashes and metrics;
- minimized failure cases with permission;
- deliberately shareable excerpts.

Keep full manuscripts in the author's project workspace unless the author explicitly chooses to bundle them.

A hash is an integrity reference, not permission to redistribute the underlying text.

## 17. CLI and artifact contract

Canonical live-campaign assets:

- `assets/live-campaign/campaign-registry.json`
- `assets/live-campaign/campaign-manifest-template.json`
- `assets/live-campaign/checkpoint-input-template.json`

Unified surface:

```bash
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign registry
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign init CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT --campaign-id ID --track TRACK --target-chapters 100
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign session-start CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT --session-id SESSION_ID
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign checkpoint CAMPAIGN_DIR CHECKPOINT_INPUT --project-root PROJECT_ROOT
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign session-end CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT --session-id SESSION_ID --outcome completed
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign verify CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign status CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign trajectory CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT
python scripts/storyctl.py campaign claim CAMPAIGN_DIR --project-root PROJECT_ROOT --level L4
```

The checkpoint command requires a previously registered session, computes artifact hashes, records the active Skill/harness hashes, snapshots probe/creative/resume/session evidence, appends a tamper-evident checkpoint hash chain, and calls existing canonical validators; it does not reimplement project/runtime/kernel business rules. Session start/end records have their own hashed index so session diversity and failed/aborted outcomes remain auditable. The harness also records its own UTC capture time and reports any large discrepancy from the author-supplied session timestamp. `trajectory` reports context-budget growth, Runtime-event growth, maintenance density, probe outcomes, checkpoint spacing, tooling eras, and the multi-axis evidence passport descriptively. It deliberately emits no composite literary or reliability score.

## 18. Research basis

This protocol adapts long-horizon evaluation ideas conservatively. Canonical metadata/status live in `external-research-ledger.md`; these stable IDs record exactly which research line supports each design choice.

- [research:ama-bench-2026] - combine realistic agent trajectories with scalable controlled stress; keep live campaign evidence distinct from synthetic stress.
- [research:ultrahorizon-2026] - stress sustained planning, memory, tool use, partial observability, and error accumulation over genuinely long horizons.
- [research:membench-2025] - separate memory effectiveness, efficiency, capacity, levels, and interaction scenarios.
- [research:mem2actbench-2026] - test whether remembered state changes future action/tool parameters rather than only explicit recall answers.
- [research:infiagent-2026] - external persistent state plus bounded active context motivates explicit state externalization for long-running workflows.
- [research:longmemeval-2025] - distinguish extraction, multi-session reasoning, temporal reasoning, updates, and abstention.
- [research:locomo-2024] - multi-session conversations expose long-range temporal and causal memory failures.
- [research:locomo-plus-2026] - test latent constraint consistency when later cues do not restate the remembered fact.
- [research:locomo-conv-2026] - conversationally implicit/composed memory use motivates observational action evidence beyond QA-style probes; treat current publication status as preprint until canonical proceedings are verified.
- [research:sentinelbench-2026] - time-evolving monitoring motivates measuring useful waiting, reaction time, and resource use separately.
- [research:odysseys-2026] - graded rubric checkpoints and explicit trajectory efficiency motivate decomposed long-horizon evidence.
- [research:temporal-flattening-2026] - reduced longitudinal semantic/cognitive-emotional variation motivates creative-trajectory drift evidence.
- [research:litbench-2026] - limited agreement of off-the-shelf LLM judges with human creative-writing preferences motivates bounded judge evidence and human review.
- **Software soak / reliability practice** - extended-operation evidence should track trends over time and preserve reproducible baselines instead of relying on one final successful state; this is general engineering practice rather than a single research citation.

Do not copy another benchmark's score into this Skill. Translate only the evaluation principles needed to diagnose real long-form creative use.

## Poetics longitudinal checks

In real longitudinal campaigns, track Project Poetics separately from prose-surface similarity.

- distinguish approved project/arc/chapter overrides from accidental drift;
- preserve do-not-optimize constraints across long context gaps;
- test whether dormant but reactivated capabilities return only when current scope calls for them;
- verify that a chapter experiment does not silently rewrite the project-level contract;
- allow project poetics to evolve through an explicit discovery/approval path rather than treating every change as failure;
- report cross-project convergence toward one Skill-favored architecture as a regression signal, not as improvement.

## Chapter 1 handoff ordering

For a project registered for a live campaign (including an assessed time-loop/branch substantial opening), register prospectively at Chapter 0 before prose. Initialization is baseline, never chapter evidence. `storyctl readiness MANIFEST READINESS --root PROJECT_ROOT --action start-chapter-1 --session-id ID` checks project health, formal live draft permission, then delegates `session-start` to this existing campaign harness. A failed permission never creates a session; a session never grants permission. The helper returns a drafting handoff, not prose. After actual writing, reconcile/commit and record canonical checkpoints under this protocol. Keep failed/aborted sessions visible.
