# Literary Meta-Core: Cross-Poetics Distinctions and Guardrails

Use this reference when planning, diagnosing, revising, or evaluating a long-form project and there is a risk of treating one successful literary mechanism as a universal rule. It owns **cross-poetics distinctions**, evidence-boundary reminders, and pluralist guardrails. It does not prescribe a single aesthetic target and does not replace project-specific poetics.

## Contents

- Anti-collapse distinctions

## 1. Core rule

This meta-core is informed by reasoned critical pluralism and category-sensitive aesthetic judgment rather than by a single literary-value function [research:booth-critical-understanding-1979] [research:walton-categories-art-1970]. Its protection of ordinary/familiar experience from automatic event- or defamiliarization-based downgrading is also research-informed [research:saito-familiar-2017]. Narrative-purpose language below is compatible with rhetorical narratology but remains a Skill design synthesis rather than a universal literary theorem [research:phelan-narrative-rhetoric-1996].

Do not optimize every novel toward the same bundle of virtues. A mechanism may be powerful without being universally desirable. Diagnose the project on the terms it has established, then apply only the craft capabilities that serve those terms.

When a quality claim appears, ask first:

- Is this a hard continuity/information constraint, a project-specific formal commitment, or only a common heuristic?
- What kind of evidence supports the claim: narratology, philosophy/aesthetics, criticism, literary history, reader research, psychology, or project-specific observation?
- Would applying the claim indiscriminately damage a valid project organized by a different poetics?

## 2. Anti-collapse distinctions

Keep at least these distinctions available during diagnosis:

- **eventfulness != narrative value** - importance may arise from event, perception, relation, recurrence, thought, atmosphere, inquiry, or formal movement.
- **progression != event escalation** - progression may be epistemic, relational, perceptual, temporal, social, formal, thematic, or deliberately stationary.
- **coherence != explanation density** - a work can be coherent without explaining every mechanism, motive, symbol, or ontology.
- **consistency != invariance** - a work may be consistent in an authorized pattern of transformation, including deliberate shifts of voice or form.
- **psychological depth != explicit introspection** - action, speech, habit, relation, omission, contradiction, and self-misreading can carry mind.
- **first-person access != psychological truth** - self-report is evidence about a mind and a narrative act, not automatic canonical diagnosis.
- **character quality != completed arc** - stability, oscillation, relapse, failed change, repetition, and opacity can be legitimate.
- **repetition != redundancy** - repetition can establish habit, ritual, baseline, evidence, form, relation, or remembered recurrence.
- **detail != clue** - a detail may function as evidence, material life, perception, atmosphere, habit, rhythm, symbol, or simply concrete presence.
- **ordinary != filler** - ordinary life can carry time, relation, attachment, social order, embodiment, and aesthetic attention directly.
- **atmosphere != scenery quantity** - atmosphere can emerge from bodies, spaces, objects, sounds, expectations, relations, institutions, and time.
- **style != ornament** - form can participate in cognition, temporality, focalization, social language, and reader activity.
- **technique != guaranteed effect** - a device does not force every reader to experience the same result.
- **formula != cliche** - inherited structures and genre conventions may remain productive; quality depends on use, not mere recurrence.
- **familiarity != attachment** - familiarity may produce affection, irritation, dread, indifference, or mixed feeling.
- **symbolic != nonliteral** - literal fictional status and symbolic force may coexist.
- **world autonomy != equal narrative attention** - offscreen life need not receive equal page time, and some projects may intentionally restrict world autonomy.
- **canonical truth != textual explicitness** - backstage certainty does not require on-page explanation.
- **reader model != canonical world model** - readers forget, compress, infer, misattribute, and update imperfectly.
- **production representation != literary ontology** - databases, graphs, timelines, state machines, and ledgers are management tools, not claims about what a novel essentially is.
- **memory != chronology** - associative temporal architecture need not be normalized into dated flashbacks.
- **social movement != level progression** - access, reputation, shame, desire and self-respect may move against one another.
- **SYSTEM UNKNOWN != AUTHORIAL OPEN != READER WITHHELD != INTERPRETIVELY OPEN** - missing operational evidence, governed fictional uncertainty, disclosure limits and interpretive remainder have different owners; none repairs broken provenance.

## 3. Evidence identity

Preserve the epistemic identity of borrowed knowledge.

- Philosophy/aesthetics supplies concepts and arguments, not population-level reader effects.
- Narratology supplies formal distinctions and models, not universal cognition.
- Experimental psychology supplies mechanisms observed under particular samples/tasks, not direct literary norms.
- Meta-analysis/review can strengthen a behavioral claim without turning it into an aesthetic command.
- Literary criticism/close reading explains a work or corpus; it does not prove all readers or all traditions behave the same way.
- Literary history/cultural study is historically situated; do not universalize one tradition's categories.
- A pressure-test work tests explanatory reach; it does not become a style template.
- Skill synthesis is a bridge proposal. Keep it revisable and do not mislabel it as consensus.

## 4. Pluralist guardrails

Never make any of the following global optimization targets without explicit project authority:

- event density;
- suspense or transportation;
- character transformation;
- psychological transparency;
- world breadth/autonomy;
- atmosphere intensity;
- stylistic foregrounding;
- readability/processing fluency;
- symbolism density;
- foreshadowing/payoff density;
- polyphony;
- formal stability;
- formal novelty;
- explanatory completeness.

A project may deliberately prioritize one, several, or none of these.

## 5. Diagnosis order

When something feels "wrong," classify the failure before revising:

1. **hard integrity failure** - contradiction, impossible access, unearned knowledge, invalid state transition, broken revision lineage;
2. **project-poetics violation** - the text breaks a formal/experiential commitment the project has actually established;
3. **unauthorized drift** - the text changes regime without project/arc/chapter authorization or a defensible emergent-development proposal;
4. **mere absence of another poetics' virtue** - not a failure by itself.

Do not repair category 4.

## 6. Anti-overcorrection checks

Before "improving" prose or architecture, ask whether the change would:

- add plot merely because a scene is quiet;
- add introspection merely because motivation is not fully verbalized;
- turn ordinary details into clues or setups;
- force a side character into a full arc;
- impose a hidden network where no hidden network is needed;
- turn theme into competing positions when the project realizes theme otherwise;
- normalize deliberate stylistic variation;
- explain an ontology the project intentionally leaves minimally specified;
- close an ending whose experiential or formal completion is intentionally open;
- convert a retelling/parody into "original worldbuilding" to satisfy an unrelated ideal.

If yes, route to `project-poetics.md` before revising.
