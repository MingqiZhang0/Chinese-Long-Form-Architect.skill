# Prose Integration Standard

Use this reference for the final integration pass when drafting or revising actual fiction. It owns sentence-level integration, exposition integration, emotional embodiment, anti-AI diagnostics, and revision order. `paragraph-architecture.md` owns semantic paragraph structure and paragraph clusters.

It does **not** redefine the specialized rules for focalization, dialogue, figurative language, environment, dynamic action, rhythm, temporal duration, endings, or theme. Route those problems to their canonical owners in `system-map.md`.

## 1. Integration hierarchy: hard integrity plus constitutive form

Do not use one linear hierarchy that always demotes voice/rhythm/form to ornament.

Protect two interacting constraint families:

### Hard semantic integrity

- user-locked canon and authorial authority;
- causal/temporal/spatial/knowledge legality;
- focalization/camera permission;
- durable state and evidence/provenance.

### Constitutive project-form commitments

When Project Poetics explicitly makes form part of the work's experience, also protect:

- authorized voice/form regime or heterogeneity;
- sentence/paragraph movement essential to temporality or cognition;
- chosen opacity/explanation threshold;
- licensed recurrence/ellipsis/fragmentation;
- do-not-optimize constraints.

A prettier sentence may never justify a hard contradiction. But a generic clarity/pace/voice-normalization edit may also be wrong if it destroys an approved constitutive formal commitment.

Use `project-poetics.md` and `literary-meta-core.md` before resolving apparent craft conflicts.

## 2. Exposition discipline

Prefer when possible:

1. consequence;
2. behavior;
3. partial inference;
4. clarification when it becomes useful.

Avoid:

- encyclopedic blocks inserted only because the author knows the lore;
- glossary dialogue;
- characters reciting shared knowledge for the reader;
- explanation immediately after an action or image already made the meaning legible;
- restating the same information in narration, internal thought, and dialogue.

If characters share a rule, let them discuss its present cost, implication, disagreement, exception, evidence, or procedural consequence rather than reciting the rule from scratch.

For information ownership and reveal fairness, use `knowledge-topology.md`. For page-density restraint, use `restraint-negative-space.md`.

## 3. Emotional embodiment

Prefer observable consequence over instant labels when the behavior can carry the feeling.

Useful evidence includes:

- altered routine;
- delayed reply;
- changed handling of an object;
- avoiding a name;
- excessive politeness;
- a practical task performed at the wrong time;
- money or time spent differently;
- a familiar response failing to appear;
- a choice that costs more than the character admits.

Do not append "he was moved / angry / afraid" when the scene already makes it clear unless the explicit self-label itself matters.

Build emotion through attachment, anticipation, uncertainty, interpretation, choice, cost, consequence, and aftermath.

For long-term psychological change, use `character-dynamics.md` and `state-inertia.md`.

## 4. Literary texture

Literary texture may come from:

- exact observation;
- recurring images whose meaning changes over time;
- temporal layering;
- irony between belief and reality;
- objects accumulating history;
- social and material detail;
- implication and restraint;
- friction between private feeling and public role.

It does not require ornate metaphor or constant lyricism. Use `figurative-language.md` when metaphor, simile, recurring imagery, source-domain fit, or symbolic overcommitment is the actual problem.

When the user cites authors, abstract transferable structural qualities. Do not imitate distinctive sentence-level style.

Concrete perception should normally precede abstract interpretation when the reader needs to see the thing first.

## 5. Daily-life integration

Do not insert "slice of life" as a quota category.

Ask instead:

- What does this person normally do before the current objective?
- What inconvenience follows from where and how they live?
- Who appears because of routine rather than plot?
- What do income, status, climate, infrastructure, family, or work make easy or hard?
- What maintenance continues regardless of the protagonist's arc?

Most ordinary details should pass without commentary. Some should remain mundane forever.

For autonomous local change and social ecology, use `world-evolution-social-ecology.md`.

## 6. Strategic intelligence on the page

Show the inputs of intelligence rather than narrator praise:

- evidence;
- uncertainty;
- preparation;
- assumptions being tested;
- unacceptable risks;
- verification;
- fallback structure;
- accepted cost.

A smart plan should survive the obvious response or establish why that response is unavailable, unseen, too costly, or inconsistent with the opponent's objective.

For formal strategic review, use `adversarial-review.md`. For councils and procedure-heavy negotiation, use `institutional-negotiation.md`.

## 7. High-pressure prose integration

During combat, pursuit, accident, collapse, or panic, keep enough state legible that the reader can reconstruct what changed.

Do not replace causality with:

- ability names;
- louder adjectives;
- escalating numbers;
- generic slow motion;
- a pile of action verbs with no changed geometry or tactical state.

Respect injuries, fatigue, counters, information, terrain, mission constraints, visibility, and bodily access.

After major action, preserve consequences rather than returning immediately to baseline.

Use `dynamic-vividness.md` for kinetic causality and `scene-architecture.md` for tactical scene structure.

## 8. Sentence rhythm and paragraph handoff

Vary sentence length according to perception, pressure, and voice rather than a visible template. Ordinary life may flow through longer syntactic units; danger may narrow clause structure; procedural language may become more qualified; exhaustion may fragment thought; intimacy may tolerate unfinished sentences or shared shorthand.

Do not manufacture cadence through repetitive long-short-long-short sequencing, habitual three-part crescendos, one-sentence-per-line formatting, or a mini-reversal at every paragraph end.

Route semantic paragraph boundaries, paragraph clusters, long-paragraph structure, dialogue paragraphing, modernist boundary misalignment, and mobile display blocks to `paragraph-architecture.md`. For expectation-level rhythm and cuts, use `rhythm-propulsion.md`.

## 9. Chinese grammar and phraseological naturalness

For Chinese fiction, load `chinese-grammar-idiom.md` when sentence-level naturalness depends on reference/zero anaphora, `不` versus `没/没有`, aspect (`了/着/过`), result/directional complements, `把/被`, `的/地/得`, connective scope, sentence-final particles, punctuation, idiom meaning/grammar/register, or fixed-expression use.

Keep one summary rule here because it is a recurring failure signature: **do not compress bounded event non-occurrence into a bare comma + `不 + verb` tail merely to create hardness or rhythm**. If the meaning is simply that the action did not happen in that moment, natural `没有/没 + verb` is usually the correct starting point. Do not suppress ordinary Chinese grammar for cosmetic terseness.

Likewise, do not use idioms, four-character cadence, explicit connectives, or sentence-final particles as generic "Chinese flavor". Each carries lexical, grammatical, discourse, register, or interpersonal information.

When uncertain, preserve meaning first and route the issue to `chinese-grammar-idiom.md`; do not perform global search-and-replace on `不`, `没有`, particles, pronouns, or idioms.

## 10. AI-pattern diagnostics

Watch for repeated clusters rather than banning isolated phrases.

Common failure signatures include:

- repeated "not X but Y" contrasts or Chinese `不是 X，而是 Y`-type corrective frames;
- clipped comma + `不 + verb` tails used to mean a bounded action simply did not happen;
- stable Chinese topic chains rewritten with an explicit `他/她/角色名` in nearly every clause despite no competing referent;
- aspect particles stamped onto verbs as tense markers rather than used for completion, continuation, experience, or change-of-state meaning;
- dialogue particles/discourse markers sprinkled across speakers as generic colloquial flavor;
- explicit logical connectives announcing relations that event order and topic continuity already make clear;
- idiom/four-character stacking used as a shortcut for literary polish, intelligence, or social status;
- habitual three-part crescendos;
- generic profundity after ordinary action;
- constant rhetorical questions;
- stock micro-expression gestures;
- vague atmosphere words replacing concrete perception;
- object -> instant simile -> exit, with no material persistence;
- every landscape element made symbolic, ominous, or plot-relevant;
- narrator translating every subtext;
- identical internal-monologue cadence across characters;
- automatic ominous final sentences;
- explanation repeated in adjacent forms;
- every intelligent speaker inheriting the narrator's polish.

Diagnose the generating habit. Do not mechanically blacklist a phrase that is natural in context. Natural `没有/没` is not itself an anti-pattern.

## 11. Revision hierarchy

Revise from high layer to low layer:

1. wrong scene selection;
2. broken causality, rule, or motivation;
3. wrong knowledge/camera access;
4. missing consequence or state inertia;
5. weak scene delta or tactical adaptation;
6. reader-load or orientation problem;
7. flat rhythm or poor time allocation;
8. exposition leakage or explanation echo;
9. missing material/social embodiment where the scene depends on it;
10. unauthorized voice/form drift (distinguish from approved scoped variation);
11. paragraph/sentence polish;
12. ornament.

Do not beautify prose that belongs to a scene the novel should not be showing.

## 12. Routing

This file is an integration layer, not a second ownership map. When a prose defect belongs to focalization, paragraph architecture, dialogue, figurative language, environment, dynamic action, time, rhythm, negative space, reveal design, theme, or closure, route to the canonical owner in `system-map.md` rather than restating its rules here.
