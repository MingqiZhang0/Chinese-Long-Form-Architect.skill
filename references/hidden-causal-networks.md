# Hidden Causal Networks, Counterfactual Exposure, and Adaptive Opposition

> SELECTABLE POETICS / CAUSAL CAPABILITY. Activate when adaptive hidden processes, covert actors, counterfactual exposure, or unseen causal networks materially organize the project. Do not add one merely to make a novel feel deep.

Use this reference for dense long-form mysteries, deep foreshadowing, autonomous offscreen antagonists, prophecy or prediction mechanics, time loops, repeated scenarios, alternate branches, strategic probing, and reveals that should make earlier scenes acquire new meaning without becoming false.

## Core principle

A deep hidden thread is not a secret answer waiting behind the plot. It is an offscreen causal process that keeps acting, learning, spending resources, changing plans, and creating residue before the reader understands what it is.

Prefer:

> hidden process -> visible consequence -> plausible surface interpretation -> intervention -> altered consequence -> deeper inference -> adaptation -> later reinterpretation

Avoid:

> mysterious clue -> static hidden answer -> reveal -> thread disappears

The strongest long-form mystery makes the world feel active before it makes the author feel clever.

## 1. Hidden threads as processes, not boxes

For every major hidden thread, distinguish:

- **state** - what is currently true;
- **actor** - who or what maintains the process;
- **objective** - what they ultimately want;
- **current plan** - what they are doing now;
- **assumptions** - what they believe about the world and opposition;
- **inputs** - what new information can change the plan;
- **fallbacks** - what they do if the current route fails;
- **clock** - what continues even if the protagonist never notices;
- **surface manifestations** - what ordinary people may see without understanding;
- **reader model** - the most plausible current interpretation available from the text.

A thread should be able to answer: "What happens next if the protagonist does nothing?"

If the answer is "nothing until the protagonist arrives," the thread is not autonomous yet.

## 2. Thread depth without mastermind inflation

Depth does not mean placing one secret villain behind another forever.

Prefer vertical reinterpretation:

1. **event layer** - what visibly happened;
2. **mechanism layer** - how it happened;
3. **strategic layer** - why this method was chosen;
4. **system layer** - what history, institution, ideology, ecology, or long-term conflict made the strategy sensible.

A strong reveal often changes the category of the question.

Examples:

- from "who attacked the school?" to "why did someone need a lockdown?";
- from "who leaked the plan?" to "why does making the plan create a detectable trace?";
- from "which official is corrupt?" to "which procedure makes corruption rational and durable?".

Do not use another hidden mastermind when a deeper mechanism, incentive, relationship, or historical constraint can provide the depth.

## 3. Hidden causal thread record

For important hidden threads, track privately:

- thread ID;
- owner or responsible system;
- long-term objective;
- immediate objective;
- current plan;
- secondary or diagnostic objective, if any;
- resources and constraints;
- relevant relationships;
- current knowledge and uncertainty;
- current theory of the protagonist or opposition;
- observed anomalies;
- adaptation threshold;
- fallback plans;
- time or resource clock;
- surface events the thread can naturally produce;
- current reader interpretation;
- clues already available;
- conditions that would expose the thread;
- conditions that would make it go quiet rather than disappear;
- likely residue if blocked;
- unresolved downstream effects.

Update the record after meaningful interference. Do not reset an antagonist to their original plan merely because the protagonist prevented one outcome.

## 4. Occurrence order versus disclosure order

Maintain two distinct chronologies for deep mysteries:

### World chronology
What actually happened, in causal order, including hidden actions and offscreen consequences.

### Disclosure chronology
When the reader learns, suspects, misreads, forgets, or reinterprets each part.

Do not design the mystery only in disclosure order. First make the world chronology causally complete, then choose where the narrative opens windows onto it.

For important hidden events record:

- actual time;
- participants;
- immediate consequence;
- records or traces created;
- who could discover those traces;
- first reader-facing manifestation;
- current surface explanation;
- later reinterpretation point.

This separation prevents the common failure where a twist exists only because the author postponed inventing its cause.

## 5. First-read surface logic and reread logic

A hidden action should normally support two coherent readings.

### First-read reading

The action makes sense from visible motives, tactics, genre expectations, local incentives, or ordinary world behavior.

### Reread reading

Later information reveals an additional function, motive, causal link, or diagnostic purpose that was present from the beginning.

Require both.

A good reread reveal says:

> "That scene meant more than I knew."

A bad reread reveal says:

> "The author withheld the only fact that could have made the scene make sense."

Use true but incomplete interpretation rather than false narration.

## 6. Counterfactual exposure

Time loops, resets, prophecy, simulations, visions, repeated investigations, reconstructed timelines, and alternate branches can expose hidden causal structure by showing different outcomes under different interventions.

Treat repeated scenarios as causal evidence, not just retries.

For each branch ask:

- what remained invariant;
- what the protagonist changed;
- what changed despite no direct intervention;
- which actors noticed the intervention;
- which actors adapted;
- what visible disaster disappeared;
- what hidden objective remained;
- what new cost or threat replaced the prevented event;
- what hypothesis the branch supports or weakens.

The narrative value of a second branch should be more than "the protagonist now knows the answer." It should reveal a different portion of the hidden system.

## 7. Branch matrix

For repeated or counterfactual scenarios, maintain a compact branch matrix.

Track:

- branch ID;
- initial conditions shared with other branches;
- intervention or changed choice;
- observed outcome;
- hidden actor response;
- invariant facts;
- changed facts;
- knowledge carried across branches, if any;
- new hypothesis;
- hypothesis disproved;
- unresolved anomaly;
- cost paid for obtaining the comparison.

Use the matrix to prevent cosmetic branches where only the victim, location, or spectacle changes while the causal model stays identical.

This **counterfactual branch matrix** is an analytical/narrative device. If an alternate line becomes a durable editable canon world line, register it as a formal canon branch under `narrative-runtime.md`; do not let the two branch concepts drift into separate sources of truth.

A useful branch changes what the reader believes causes the event.

## 8. Prevention is not resolution

Never assume:

> event prevented = cause removed

After intervention, check separately:

- objective removed?;
- capability removed?;
- actor removed?;
- opportunity removed?;
- detection risk increased?;
- plan merely delayed?;
- target changed?;
- method changed?;
- hidden actor now knows someone interfered?;
- new evidence created?;
- new false confidence created?

A peaceful branch may be more dangerous than a violent one if the antagonist has learned that their plan is being observed.

Silence is a state, not proof of safety.

## 9. Adaptive opposition

Important opponents should run a loop:

> observe -> interpret -> update belief -> choose response -> observe again

They do not need perfect information. They need memory.

Track what they can reasonably infer from:

- repeated interception;
- suspicious timing;
- improbable coincidence;
- changed defenses;
- missing resources;
- false alarms;
- unusual questions;
- behavior that implies foreknowledge;
- traces created by powers, surveillance, magic, technology, or procedure.

Let them form competing hypotheses before reaching the correct one.

Example progression:

1. coincidence;
2. internal leak;
3. compromised communication channel;
4. unknown intelligence capability;
5. predictive or supernatural mechanism;
6. deliberate testing and counterplay.

Do not skip directly to the correct explanation unless the opponent has prior domain knowledge.

## 10. Camouflaged experiments and probing attacks

A competent adversary may use real operations to learn about the protagonist.

The experiment must not look like an experiment on first read.

Require **first-order utility**:

- the attack can still damage a target;
- the feint can still draw forces away;
- the changed route can still reduce risk;
- the compartmentalization can still protect secrecy;
- the false rumor can still create political pressure;
- the delayed strike can still improve tactical position.

Then add **second-order diagnostic value**:

- test whether a defense reacts before information could travel;
- test which plan variant triggers a response;
- test whether the leak sits at command, staff, courier, or field level;
- test whether the opponent reacts to intent, order, movement, or execution;
- test whether a supernatural or technical observation leaves a trace.

Do not stage clean laboratory A/B tests unless the setting makes them natural. Vary one useful operational choice inside otherwise credible strategy.

### Camouflage test

Before using a probing action, ask:

- If the hidden experiment were deleted, would the visible operation still make sense?
- Would a first-time reader call the action normal strategy rather than "the author is testing a rule"?
- Does the adversary gain something even if no diagnostic information is learned?
- Is the variable change small enough to hide inside ordinary tactical variation?
- Can the later reveal reconstruct what was being tested from details already present?

If not, redesign the action.

## 11. Information powers as actions

Prediction, prophecy, mind reading, remote viewing, magical detection, surveillance, simulation, and retrocognition should not be treated as consequence-free camera access.

For every information power define:

- what exactly can be queried;
- precision;
- temporal or spatial range;
- target complexity;
- uncertainty and branching;
- resource or bodily cost;
- cooldown or recovery;
- detectability;
- traces or side channels;
- whether observation changes the observed system;
- whether targets can adapt after learning the capability exists;
- whether false inputs can poison the result;
- whether repeated queries create a signature;
- what happens under self-reference;
- what institutions would do if such a capability were known.

Information advantages are most interesting when they create new strategic problems instead of eliminating uncertainty.

## 12. Prediction, prophecy, and observational side effects

A reusable pattern is:

> observation of a future state creates a detectable or causal trace in that state

The trace may be physical, magical, cognitive, statistical, social, or institutional.

This creates a strategic loop:

1. predictor observes likely future;
2. target or environment receives a trace;
3. sensitive target notices an anomaly;
4. target changes behavior;
5. the original prediction becomes less reliable;
6. both sides must reason about who knows that the other knows.

Do not make the trace automatically reveal the observer, content, or exact time of prediction. Partial detectability preserves uncertainty.

Useful design dimensions include:

- trace strength grows with precision;
- trace strength grows as predicted time approaches;
- unstable futures cost more or leave stronger traces;
- repeated observation creates a recognizable signature;
- broad forecasts are safer but less actionable;
- precise tactical forecasts are powerful but easier to detect;
- prediction can become a signaling channel or bait once the trace is understood.

This converts prophecy from a spoiler machine into an information-war mechanic.

## 13. Self-reference and dangerous self-observation

If a predictive system treats the observer as part of the world being predicted, self-observation can create special problems.

Possible rule families include:

- recursive recalculation because learning the prediction changes the observer's future;
- escalating cost because each updated forecast changes the state again;
- information collapse or undefined result;
- lethal stabilization, where death is the only state that ends recursion;
- institutional taboo developed around a real but poorly understood mechanism.

Use only if established by the project. Do not invent a self-reference exception at the climax.

If self-prediction is lethal in canon, seed both the cultural rule and the mechanical consequences early enough that the later explanation feels like deepening, not replacement.

## 14. Cultural explanation versus actual mechanism

A world may contain several explanations for the same supernatural rule:

- folk saying;
- religious taboo;
- professional rule of thumb;
- academic model;
- state doctrine;
- the actual underlying mechanism.

These do not need to match perfectly.

This is useful when drawing inspiration from real divination, omen, fate, or ritual traditions. Treat broad cultural motifs as inspiration unless a specific historical source establishes a universal doctrine. Do not present a synthesized fictional rule as if it were the single authentic rule of a diverse real tradition.

A culturally familiar explanation can hide a hard world rule in plain sight, but the later mechanical explanation must remain compatible with the earlier cultural behavior.

## 15. Suspicion, betrayal, and real secondary secrets

When a character is falsely suspected of betrayal, do not make every suspicious fact evaporate after exoneration.

Prefer:

- the accusation is wrong;
- several suspicious behaviors are real;
- those behaviors belong to a different secret, obligation, shame, affiliation, cost, or hidden project;
- clearing one mystery opens another without undoing character complexity.

This avoids the "all clues were innocent coincidences" failure.

The false theory should be rational from available evidence, not merely emotionally convenient.

## 16. Long-horizon clue reactivation

Long-form foreshadowing should survive real reader memory limits.

Use a loose cycle:

> seed -> cool -> reactivate -> transform -> payoff

A reactivation should have a current-world reason to appear. Do not insert a reminder only because the author fears the reader forgot.

Useful reactivation channels include:

- a repeated institutional phrase;
- a symbol used in a new context;
- a changed location or object;
- a side character remembering the event differently;
- a procedural consequence;
- a recurring injury, debt, habit, route, or social taboo;
- a second event with the same hidden mechanism but a different surface form.

Prefer varied reactivation over verbatim repetition. The reader should feel recognition before receiving explanation.

Do not make a major payoff depend on remembering one isolated proper noun from hundreds of chapters earlier with no reactivation.

## 17. Payoff geometry

A deep thread can pay off at several scales:

### Local payoff
Explains one event or saves one immediate problem.

### Relational payoff
Changes trust, loyalty, guilt, fear, or obligation.

### Interpretive payoff
Changes the meaning of earlier scenes.

### Strategic payoff
Changes what actors now know about one another and how they plan.

### Systemic payoff
Reveals a deeper rule, institution, historical structure, or long conflict.

### Residual payoff
Leaves new problems after the reveal: retaliation, adaptation, public misunderstanding, institutional response, or a surviving objective.

Do not require every reveal to hit all levels. Major long-form reveals should usually do more than provide a name.

## 18. World remainder

Not every side thread should converge on the protagonist or final conspiracy.

Preserve some processes whose value is to prove that other people have lives:

- marriages;
- debts;
- local elections;
- career changes;
- family disputes;
- minor rivalries;
- businesses opening or failing;
- migrations;
- ordinary illness and recovery;
- friendships that cool or deepen;
- organizations changing for reasons unrelated to the protagonist.

A world feels larger when some causal lines cross the main plot, some only brush it, and some never do.

The criterion is not "serves the main plot." The criterion is "belongs to the world and changes someone or something honestly."

## 19. Audit

Before locking a deep hidden-thread arc, ask:

- Does the hidden process continue if the protagonist does nothing?
- What does the hidden actor do after failure?
- What have they learned from prior interference?
- Does a prevented disaster leave an objective or actor alive?
- Can quietness represent adaptation rather than safety?
- Does each major branch reveal a different causal fact?
- Are invariant facts tracked across branches?
- Are counterfactual comparisons doing explanatory work rather than replaying spectacle?
- Does the first-read interpretation remain reasonable?
- Does the reread interpretation add meaning without making prior narration false?
- Are probing actions useful as normal strategy even without the experiment?
- Does an information power have costs, detectability, counters, and institutional implications?
- Are false suspicions supported by real evidence?
- Does exoneration preserve other real secrets where appropriate?
- Is thread depth coming from causal structure rather than endless hidden masterminds?
- Are some side processes allowed to remain independent of the protagonist?
- After the reveal, what continues moving?

## 20. Causal sufficiency and responsibility (P0-4)

A causal network is not sufficient merely because every node has an incoming arrow. Presence, association, aftermath, intelligence and eventual success do not establish a responsibility-bearing intervention. Audit the strength of the claimed causal role, not graph size or literary merit. Hidden Causal Networks remains the sole semantic owner; State Validation checks the structured subset.

Activate this audit for death/major accident responsibility, the hidden mystery core, deliberate institutional manipulation, high-stakes betrayal, major irreversible outcomes, mastermind attribution or another explicitly high-risk consequential event. Ordinary walking, texture and incidental objects need no record. Moral/legal responsibility, real-world causal proof and a universal deterministic universe are outside this contract. Use abstract mechanism classes; no operational harm methods belong in examples or fixtures.

### Optional Story State view

Use `causal_events` inside the existing Story State Bundle, rather than a second causal database. The optional subset is `assets/project-schemas/causal-event.schema.json`; project-format versions do not change. Existing thread records already own plans, assumptions, inputs and fallbacks. A typed event references those semantics at the granularity necessary to audit a declared outcome:

```json
{
  "id": "E", "causal_version": "1.0.0", "high_risk": true,
  "active_use": true, "causal_role": "PRIMARY_CAUSE",
  "responsible_agent": "A", "intentional": true,
  "plan_type": "PREPLANNED", "mastery_claim": false,
  "mechanism_class": "CONDITION_CREATION", "outcome": "Y",
  "responsible_action_or_omission": {
    "id": "ACT", "actor": "A", "kind": "ACTION", "at": 5,
    "description": "Abstract intervention changes causal opportunity",
    "outcome_ref": "Y", "causal_relevance": true,
    "intent": true, "mechanism_awareness": true,
    "knowledge_refs": ["F"], "provenance": "Project action record"
  },
  "conditions": [{
    "id": "C", "classification": "KNOWN_STABLE", "necessity": "NECESSARY",
    "fact_ref": "F", "stability_basis": "Prior stable observation",
    "provenance": "Project condition record"
  }],
  "counterfactual_without_action": {
    "result": "OUTCOME_MATERIALLY_CHANGED", "provenance": "Branch comparison"
  },
  "responsibility_basis": {
    "action_ref": "ACT", "condition_refs": ["C"], "outcome_ref": "Y",
    "provenance": "Project chain comparison"
  }
}
```

`F` must exist in canonical `facts`/`research_dependencies`, with legal `knowledge_state` acquisition before action time. The example is a shape illustration, not standalone proof. Record a specific outcome: changing an intermediate opportunity must not be silently promoted into determining every downstream consequence.

### Intervention, omission and claim strength

`ACTION` requires actor, identity, time, description, causal relevance, outcome linkage and provenance. `PRESENCE`, `ASSOCIATION` and `AFTERMATH` are readable diagnostic kinds but cannot support responsibility. An intentional intervention additionally requires intent and mechanism awareness. `ACCIDENTAL_ACTION`/`ACCIDENTAL_CAUSE` does not require intent or foreseeability.

`DELIBERATE_OMISSION` additionally requires capability, opportunity, deliberate intent, risk awareness and legally acquired relevant knowledge references. Mere inactivity never implies deliberate omission. `OMISSION_CAUSE` must point to that intervention kind. These declarations remain evidence to review, not machine proof of psychological intent.

`responsibility_basis` binds the exact action, outcome and complete condition ID set. Free-text explanation supplements these links; it does not replace them. Supported roles are PRIMARY_CAUSE, CONTRIBUTING_CAUSE, CONDITION_PROVIDER, TRIGGER, ENABLER, OMISSION_CAUSE, ACCIDENTAL_CAUSE, OVERDETERMINED_CAUSE, INCIDENTAL and NOT_CAUSALLY_RESPONSIBLE. The last two cannot carry an engineered/mastery claim without an actual intervention.

### Counterfactual audit

Ask: without this intervention, would this particular outcome happen essentially unchanged? A PRIMARY_CAUSE requires OUTCOME_UNLIKELY or OUTCOME_MATERIALLY_CHANGED with provenance. OUTCOME_UNCHANGED/STILL_LIKELY cannot silently retain primary attribution. Contribution/enabling can pass with a documented material RISK, TIMING, MECHANISM or OPPORTUNITY change (`material_effect`, `effect_basis`). This audits contribution without requiring guaranteed prevention.

OVERDETERMINED may pass a weaker role when `other_sufficient_causes` describes another independent, sufficient intervention with agent, action_ref, matching outcome_ref and provenance. It does not justify a sole/primary cause declaration. Unrelated arrows and a cause-name string are insufficient.

UNKNOWN/OPEN preserves ambiguity only with `closure: PRESERVE_OPEN`, no definite responsibility/mastery claim, and a review warning. CLOSED or definite responsibility alongside an open counterfactual is an error. A warning is not a fully closed causal proof. The project's Reader Contract/Project Poetics decides whether a final mystery requires unique closure; the validator does not impose uniqueness on all fiction.

### Preconditions and contingency

Each condition has identity, provenance, classification and necessity (NECESSARY, SUPPORTING, INCIDENTAL or BACKGROUND). Classification is independent of narrative salience:

- CONTROLLED links `controlled_by` to the responsible actor, `control_action_ref` to the intervention, `causal_relevance: true`, and the condition ID to the intervention's `sets_conditions`.
- KNOWN_STABLE needs `stability_basis` and a canonical `fact_ref`, acquired legally before the intervention.
- REASONABLY_EXPECTED needs `expectation_basis` (routine/institution/repeated pattern) and legally acquired `fact_ref`; expectation is not certainty.
- UNKNOWN/RANDOM is allowed. Necessary unresolved conditions affect attribution; incidental/background randomness does not.

State Validation reuses P0-3 `temporal_epistemic_issues` for backend actor uses, including time, channel, provenance, fact availability and research applicability. It does not create a Knowledge owner, infer knowledge from success or export a hidden fact to Writer. Canonical author-only facts can support private actor knowledge; Writer disclosure remains independently controlled by the existing Context Compiler. P0-1 still governs committed high-impact premises before dependency; causal records cannot commit them.

### Mastermind attribution, branches and chance

PREPLANNED describes a prior plan. ADAPTIVE/OPPORTUNISTIC describes response after observation, not foreknowledge. CHANCE_DRIVEN carries no engineered/mastery attribution. Nonintentional chance-driven tragedy with several random factors can pass without a responsible agent or intervention.

For a PREPLANNED primary, mastery or definite responsibility attribution, unresolved NECESSARY UNKNOWN/RANDOM conditions in distinct explicitly independent groups trigger MASTERMIND_INFLATION. `independent_group` and `independence_basis` are reviewed structural declarations: shared/correlated sources use one group. Missing independence does not become assumed independence; strong attribution requires review and blocks clean validation. This is not a numerical coincidence quota. Stable conditions, incidental noise, claimed strength and supported alternative paths change the result.

Existing `fallbacks` are mirrored only when necessary to discharge a specific contingency. Each typed alternative declares kind (FALLBACK/ADAPTIVE), `covers_conditions`, matching outcome_ref, provenance, a real `action`, and acquired `fact_ref` supporting the alternative mechanism. A FALLBACK must be planned by original intervention time. An ADAPTIVE response additionally must follow the condition's `resolved_at`, bind its `resolution_fact_ref` through `observed_fact_refs`, and legally acquire that observation before responding. Merely changing the plan label or adding "has fallback" cannot discharge a condition. Removing coverage restores the unresolved dependency.

A bounded uncertain plan without a mastery claim can pass with `bounded_chance: true` and `chance_basis`; no fabricated probabilities are required. Correlated uncertainties collapse to one declared source group. A coherent plan may fail (`plan_result: FAILED`); a successful plan may still fail this audit. Outcome success never retroactively proves control/acquisition. This limited validator does not simulate alternative routes or establish real statistical independence.

### Legacy, privacy and limits

Unversioned archived `causal_events` are LEGACY_UNKNOWN_SUFFICIENCY warnings, not automatically insufficient history. A legacy record promoted with `active_use: true` into a high-risk claim must satisfy current checks. Do not rewrite old corpora, campaign evidence or frozen prose; no bulk migration is required. Absent `causal_events` creates no obligation.

Keep this view backend/author-only. Never put a secret answer into a Writer mission as "do not reveal X caused Y". Causal diagnostics carry opaque stable codes and optional scene ownership, without interpolating hidden agents/actions/facts. Apply normal secret-taint and disclosure compilation to any safe derivative. Formal validation audits structured claims, not arbitrary prose, actual probabilities, ultimate counterfactual truth, legal/moral blame or literary quality.

### Surgical hardening invariants (P0-4-H / P0-4-I)

**P0-4-H — Structural Claim Strength Cannot Be Laundered.** `derive_effective_causal_claim` treats PREPLANNED plus PRIMARY_CAUSE, mastery or definite responsibility as an outcome engineering claim even when optional intentional/mastery metadata is missing or false. Explicit false intent conflicts with that strong prior-outcome plan (`causal.plan_intent_conflict`); missing intent cannot suppress contingency or intervention-basis checks. This never infers acquired knowledge from planning. Accidental or chance-driven physical primary causes without a strong prior-outcome plan remain legal; ADAPTIVE/OPPORTUNISTIC still require observed and acquired support.

**P0-4-I — Causal References Must Resolve.** `other_sufficient_causes[].action_ref` resolves through existing `responsible_action_or_omission.id` records across the whole canonical `causal_events` view, independent of event order. No second action database or reference field is introduced. Each reference must uniquely identify a validated responsibility-bearing intervention, match its actual actor and actual event/action outcome, and retain independent/sufficient/provenance support. Every declared other cause is checked, even if a separate material-effect argument exists. PRESENCE/ASSOCIATION/AFTERMATH, irrelevant actions, self-reference, reciprocal/circular proofs, invalid acquisition, open or archived unvalidated sources cannot supply sufficiency. Source failures propagate to dependent references; diagnostics remain opaque.

All declared intervention IDs, including branch action IDs, reserve identity for ambiguity checks; duplicate IDs in an active audited record fail. A planned fallback/adaptive alternative alone is not an executed sufficient cause: represent its realized intervention in an existing causal event before using it as another sufficient cause. Missing/nonexistent or ambiguous references require enrichment rather than prose substitution. Archived unversioned records still read with LEGACY_UNKNOWN_SUFFICIENCY warnings; active high-risk proofs require canonical resolution. No bulk migration or project-version change is required.

### Referenced source strength (P0-4-J)

**P0-4-J — Resolved Reference Does Not Imply Semantic Eligibility.** Canonical source semantics constrain incoming `other_sufficient_causes` claims. Resolution, relevance and local validity do not themselves establish independent sufficiency; claimant `sufficient: true` is an assertion, not evidence. Intent never overrides INCIDENTAL or NOT_CAUSALLY_RESPONSIBLE. Local `attributed` behavior is unchanged: an intentional act can remain incidental to this outcome.

The centralized `source_supports_sufficient_cause` capability policy applies after identity/actor/outcome checks and successful local plus recursive source validation. All schema roles are covered explicitly; unknown/new roles fail closed. Existing responsibility_basis binds action, outcome and complete condition set; preconditions, provenance and P0-3 acquisitions must still pass. This is an AND requirement, not a replacement for reference integrity.

| Source role | Sufficient-source capability under the current structured contract |
|---|---|
| PRIMARY_CAUSE | Closed, provenance-backed OUTCOME_UNLIKELY or OUTCOME_MATERIALLY_CHANGED comparison for the bound outcome; no material-effect-only qualification. |
| CONTRIBUTING_CAUSE | Same outcome-bound support is required (the existing B2-R representation). The role alone, risk contribution or intermediate effect is insufficient. |
| CONDITION_PROVIDER | Same outcome-bound support; provision of a condition alone is insufficient. |
| TRIGGER | Same outcome-bound support; triggering an intermediate change alone is insufficient. |
| ENABLER | Same outcome-bound support; opportunity creation alone is insufficient. |
| OMISSION_CAUSE | Same outcome-bound support plus existing deliberate-omission capability/opportunity/intent/knowledge obligations. |
| ACCIDENTAL_CAUSE | Same outcome-bound support; intent and foreseeability are not required. |
| OVERDETERMINED_CAUSE | Same outcome-bound support, or explicit OVERDETERMINED counterfactual with successful acyclic recursive source closure. An incoming reference cannot declare the source's own redundancy. |
| INCIDENTAL | Never eligible, including intentional actions. |
| NOT_CAUSALLY_RESPONSIBLE | Never eligible, including intentional actions. |

Any source `material_effect` (RISK/TIMING/MECHANISM/OPPORTUNITY) qualifies the comparison as limited contribution, so it cannot establish this stronger claim even if `result` says OUTCOME_MATERIALLY_CHANGED. OUTCOME_UNCHANGED/STILL_LIKELY and UNKNOWN/OPEN cannot become independent sufficiency through incoming metadata. PRESERVE_OPEN source closure cannot certify a closed proof. Use the exact final outcome, not an intermediate opportunity. There is no new field for source_role or sufficiency, no second truth source and no project version change.

This preserves the existing contract's strongest structured outcome comparisons, including the CONTRIBUTING_CAUSE B2-R reverse; it does not mathematically infer sufficiency from necessity or authenticate prose provenance. Such declarations still need semantic review. Archived legacy records remain readable with unknown-sufficiency warnings; absent/unknown roles cannot silently certify a new active high-risk sufficient-cause reference. Enrich only the active proof, without migrating history.

### Causal path identity and actor binding (P0-4-K)

**P0-4-K — Causal Path Independence Is Orthogonal to Actor Identity.** Overdetermination requires another distinct canonical sufficient intervention/path, not another person. The same actor may perform two independently sufficient interventions. Different actors do not themselves prove distinctness, independence or sufficiency.

`other_sufficient_causes[].agent` must still match the resolved canonical intervention's actor. No inequality with the claimant's responsible_agent is required. Intervention distinctness is enforced by unique canonical action IDs, no self-reference/ambiguity and non-circular source closure; independent/sufficient declarations and provenance remain required. P0-4-J source eligibility and all P0-3 knowledge obligations still apply. Neither actor equality nor unequal action IDs supplies missing independence evidence.

In the current representation each causal event has one realized responsible_action_or_omission; fallback/adaptive actions reserve IDs but are planned alternatives, not separately realized sources.
